Know your sunscreen

Sunscreen first. Not makeup.

Minhwa is tinted, but it is not foundation. It exists to protect your skin from the UV that causes dark spots.

  1. The cause

    Why dark spots appear

    UV light tells your skin to make more melanin. On melanin-rich skin this shows up as dark spots, patches and uneven tone, and old marks get darker.

  2. The job

    What Minhwa does

    SPF 50+ and PA++++ block UVB and UVA, the trigger. Worn every day, it helps prevent new pigmentation and keeps existing marks from getting darker.

  3. The tint

    Why it is tinted

    Mineral filters can look white on deeper skin. The tint is there so the sunscreen disappears into your skin, not to cover it.

Minhwa is

  • Daily UV protection
  • A way to prevent pigmentation
  • For every day, including cloudy days

Minhwa is not

  • Foundation or concealer
  • A treatment that removes existing spots
  • Something you wear only on sunny days

How to use

Three steps, every morning

  1. 1

    Last step of your morning

    Apply after your moisturiser, before makeup.

  2. 2

    Use enough

    About two finger-lengths for face and neck. Also cover arms, legs and any skin the sun reaches. Blend until the tint melts in.

  3. 3

    Reapply

    Every 2 hours when you are outside, and after sweating, swimming or wiping your face.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is it makeup or foundation?

No. It is a sunscreen. The tint only stops the mineral filters from looking white on deeper skin. It does not cover spots.

Will it remove my dark spots?

Sunscreen does not remove spots. It blocks the UV that causes them, which helps prevent new pigmentation and stops existing marks from getting darker. Use it every day for the best results.

Will it leave a white cast?

It is tinted with iron oxide pigments and shade-matched on Southern African skin, so it should not leave a visible white or grey layer on deeper skin tones.

Is it a mineral or chemical sunscreen?

Mineral. The UV filter is zinc oxide.

Can I wear it under makeup?

Yes. Apply it as the last step of your skincare and let it set for a minute before makeup.

How often should I reapply?

Every 2 hours when you are outdoors, and after sweating, swimming or towel drying.
